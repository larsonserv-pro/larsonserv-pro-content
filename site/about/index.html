About Me

I am a hard-working achiever aiming to please others in the most selfless ways possible. I strive for perfection in all that I do. I place your needs before mine and help all I can. I seek truth, spread love, and I want to be remembered as the person who positively changed the way the world treats each other.

I continually keep this in mind in all things I do, to be the most impactful I can be in every decision I make and thing I accomplish.

As I have encountered many different situations in my life, I have also learned many things. I apply what I learn from these situations and turn them into goals working toward self-improvement.

I am a positive person who loves to solve problems and create solutions that make a real impact. Relationships are important to me, and I love networking and building lasting connections.

Good networking and collaboration are important to my academic and professional development, and will be used greatly in many different ways for the rest of my life.

My greatest success comes from many minor -- and some great -- failures. My life's challenges are what have made me who I am today.
