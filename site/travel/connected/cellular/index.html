Telephone calls, texting (SMS), and data while traveling

Do you want to know how I stay digitally connected when I travel around the world?

Trust me when I tell you I know how hard it is to switch cellular companies -- I was with Nextel in 1996, with the walkie-talkie push-to-talk button on the side of the phone, before they got bought out by Sprint in 2005. I stayed loyal to Sprint for 14 years despite my occasional frustrations and outrageous bills.

After countless frustrations with Sprint's coverage, and my relentless friends telling me T-Mobile was the one to use, I did my own research and made the leap to T-Mobile in 2019 -- one year before they bought Sprint anyway.

T-Mobile is hands down the best mobile carrier in the USA. My SIM card works in all the countries I have traveled to -- 210+ destinations, with unlimited data and texting the moment you arrive (standard speed approximately 128Kbps, calls $.25/min after 5GB high-speed data). Connect, share, and text abroad with Magenta® plan benefits -- no setup or data-roaming charges.

Your phone just works in Mexico and Canada too. Magenta plans also offer in-flight text messaging plus WiFi on Gogo-enabled flights, so I don't miss a thing even at 30,000 feet.

Customer service has been top-notch, and I am continually surprised at all the benefits I get for all the crazy adventures and traveling I do. T-Mobile can also help cover your switching fees. Follow my link to get started.
